## Strengths
- Strong, accurate, current regulatory content (SDF USD 100/night discount to Aug 2027, 5% GST from Jan 2026, permit rules).
- Clear personalised-travel positioning; good SEO-friendly long-form copy.
- WhatsApp link already present.

## Problems found
1. **Built on Google Sites** — template look, no real branding, no custom interactions, limited SEO/performance control.
2. **No prices at all** — every package says "Price on request". Biggest conversion killer.
3. **Fixed-departure dates are stale** (Sept–Nov **2024**) and there is no live availability or flight info.
4. **No flight details anywhere** (airline, route, timings) despite "Fly In" products.
5. **No online enquiry/booking flow** — the contact page has no working form on the page itself.
6. **Inconsistent naming**: "Land of Thunder Dragon" is 6N/7D in the menu but 8N/9D on the home page; "Discover Bhutan 10N/11D" appears on the home page but has no page.
7. **Outdated details** on fixed-departure pages: "Covid vaccination certificate" is still listed as an entry requirement.
8. Itinerary pages use Title-Case-Every-Word text and mixed formats (some day-by-day pages are very long, others short).
9. No testimonials/social proof on the site itself; no photos visible in the extracted content.
10. The owner has no way to update dates/prices without editing Google Sites pages by hand.

## What the new site adds
- Modern responsive design, WhatsApp floating button + per-package/per-departure pre-filled messages.
- Filterable package catalogue with prices, highlights and next departure.
- **Departures & flights board**: date, origin city, airline, flight no., route, timings, seats, status.
- Package detail pages with itinerary timeline and **cost estimator** (package + SDF).
- Visa/SDF/GST quick facts and FAQ accordion (content taken from the live site).
- **Admin panel** (`admin.html`) to edit flights, departure dates, seats, statuses, package prices, bulk % price change, contact settings and banner; backup/import/export and a publish file.

## Business model (for the rebuild)
Inbound destination travel agency (B2C, Mumbai) selling **land packages** (hotels, transfers, guide, permits) for Bhutan to Indian travellers, plus group fixed departures with Mumbai–Paro return flights, pure-veg/Jain meals and a female guide. Revenue comes from per-person package pricing; leads arrive by phone/WhatsApp, so the new site is built around fast enquiry capture, WhatsApp hand-off, visible pricing, live departures/seats and an owner-run CRM.
