    Questions

    Before you apply.

    What documents will I need?

    Usually identity proof (Aadhaar or PAN), address proof, recent bank statements and income proof such as salary slips or an ITR. Your advisor sends the exact checklist for your product.

    Does enquiring hurt my credit score?

    Sending us an enquiry does not trigger a hard credit check. A lender may run one only when you choose to proceed with that lender.

    Do I pay Arthvan anything?

    No. Our advice is free to you and we are paid by lending partners. Any processing fee a lender charges is shown to you before you accept.

    How fast can I get a decision?

    Many personal loans and cards are decided within 24 to 48 hours of complete documents. Home and property loans need valuation and legal checks, so they take longer.

    Can I repay early?

    Yes, though terms differ by lender and loan type. We list prepayment and foreclosure charges next to each offer.
