FAQ

Questions, answered

What documents do I need?

Typically identity proof (Aadhaar/PAN), address proof, recent bank statements and income proof such as salary slips or ITR. Your advisor will share an exact checklist for your product.

Does checking eligibility affect my credit score?

Submitting an enquiry with us does not trigger a hard credit check. A lender may run one only when you choose to proceed with a specific application.

Do you charge a fee for your service?

Our advisory is free for customers; we are compensated by lending partners. Any processing fees charged by the lender are disclosed to you upfront.

How long does approval take?

Many personal loans and cards are decided within 24–48 hours of complete documents. Home and property loans take longer because of valuation and legal checks.

Can I prepay or foreclose my loan?

Yes — terms vary by lender and loan type. We highlight prepayment and foreclosure charges before you accept an offer.
