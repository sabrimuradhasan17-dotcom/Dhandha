Before you go

Visa, permit & fee essentials

🇮🇳 Indian citizens

No visa. An Entry Permit with a valid passport (6 months) or voter ID. Pay Nu. 1,200 SDF per person per night.

🌍 International

Approved Bhutan e-visa before arrival. SDF is USD 100 per person per night (half-price until 31 Aug 2027).

🧾 GST & insurance

5% GST on tour services. Travel insurance is mandatory for the whole trip.

Rules change — we confirm current requirements with the Department of Tourism and Immigration when you book.
