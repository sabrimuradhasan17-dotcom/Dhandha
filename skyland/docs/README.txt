SKYLAND TOURS & TRAVELS - WEBSITE PREVIEW
=========================================

1. Unzip this folder (keep all files together).
2. Double-click  index.html  to open the website preview in Chrome, Edge or Safari.
3. Click "Try the admin panel demo" at the top, or open  admin/index.html  directly.
   Press "Enter demo admin" to look around the admin panel.

Notes
- This is a preview. Enquiries are not sent, and changes you make in the admin demo
  stay only in your own browser.
- Prices show "Price on request" and contact details are blank until they are entered.
- Photos are from Unsplash (free licence); see the Photo credits page.
