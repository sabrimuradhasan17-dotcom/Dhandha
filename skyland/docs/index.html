Good to know

Common questions

How do I book a trip with Skyland Tours & Travels?
Send an enquiry through the website or message us on WhatsApp with your destination, dates and number of travellers. We reply with a day-wise itinerary and a quotation, and your booking is confirmed once the advance is received.
Can I customise a package?
Yes. Every package on this site is a starting point. We can change hotels, add or remove destinations, adjust the pace or build a trip from scratch around your dates and budget.
Are flights and train tickets included?
Packages cover your stay, local transport and sightseeing as listed in the inclusions. Flights and train tickets are not included unless a package says so, but we can arrange them for you on request.
What is the best time to visit India?
It depends on the region. North and central India are best from October to March, the Himalayas from March to June and September to October, Ladakh from June to September, and the south and the islands from October to March or May. See our Best Time to Visit guide for details.
Do I need a permit for any of the destinations?
Some areas need permits, such as parts of Ladakh, Sikkim and the North-East. Permit rules depend on the destination and your nationality and can change, so we confirm the current requirements and arrange permits wherever possible.
Which ID do I need to carry?
Every traveller should carry an original government photo ID, such as an Aadhaar card, passport, voter ID or driving licence. Foreign nationals need their passport and a valid visa.
